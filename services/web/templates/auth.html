How risky is your portfolio, really?

  • Risk metrics Volatility, correlation, sector exposure, drawdown and Value at Risk from 21 years of NIFTY50 data
  • Machine learning Random Forest, Gradient Boosting and XGBoost combined into one ensemble prediction
  • Stress testing Stress tests: market crash, sector crashes, oil shock, and replays of 2008 and COVID-19
